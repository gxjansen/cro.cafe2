## About Slobodan Manić

Slobodan "Sani" Manić has spent more than 15 years in website optimization. He is CXL certified in CRO, a WordPress Core contributor, and built one of the first A/B testing extensions for WooCommerce back in 2014. His career path runs from CTO at Search Engine Journal, through Klarna integration work at Krokedil, to growth strategy at Maksimer, and now into building his own products.

Today he co-founds **Web Performance Tools** (performance audits and monitoring) and **PodPacer** (AI-powered podcast planning and production), and hosts the **No Hacks Podcast** — over 220 episodes since 2021, with weekly long-form interviews and short "Snacks" episodes. He also co-organizes **Future of eXperimentation** with Ton Wesseling and Bjarn Brunenberg, and won the Experimentation Elite Community Contribution Award in 2025.

His current thesis is summed up in **Machine-First Architecture**, the framework he published in 2024–2025: your next million visitors won't be human, so websites need to be designed for AI agents as well as people. The framework covers four pillars — Identity, Structure, Content, and Interaction — and is the basis for what he calls Agent Experience Optimization (AXO). He cites the data: 69% of Google searches now end without a click, and GPTBot's share of verified bot traffic grew from 4.7% to 11.7% of verified bot traffic in a single year.

What makes Sani worth listening to is that he is skeptical without being dismissive. He uses AI tools daily, builds with them, and will tell you exactly where the hype falls apart. His methodology — "find friction, fix it, measure, repeat" — applies as cleanly to AI adoption as it does to checkout flows.
