## About Jim Sterne

Jim Sterne has spent 45 years in sales and marketing. He started out selling business computers to companies that had never owned one in the 1980s, consulted and keynoted about online marketing in the 1990s, and has spent the last 30 years focused on how organizations measure digital success.

He founded the **Marketing Analytics Summit** in 2002 and co-founded the **Digital Analytics Association** in 2004. He has written 13 books on digital marketing and analytics, including *Artificial Intelligence for Marketing: Practical Applications*. His latest, co-authored with **Tom Davenport**, is *The New Science of Customer Relationships: Delivering the One-to-One Promise with AI* (2025).

In late 2024 he co-founded **Coastal Intelligence**, an AI think tank and community initiative in Santa Barbara focused on helping local businesses, researchers, and organizations adopt AI thoughtfully. He also runs **Data Driven Leaders Studio**, a cohort-based mastermind and intensive program for analytics and AI strategy.

His current thesis: the competitive moat in AI isn't access to models — it's proprietary, well-curated data and the human judgment to use it well. The analyst role is shifting from report generator to certifier of systems.

You can follow his thinking in his LinkedIn newsletter **Full Sterne Ahead**.
