CRO.CAFE Podcast revival? In a mini-series ahead of [Experimentation Elite's Space Academy](https://experimentationelite.com/) event (May 6/7, London), I'm interviewing some of the speakers. Starting with Iqbal Ali.

Besides creating cartoons ([Tech Bro Tales](https://iqbalacomics.com/tech-bro-tales/)), Iqbal consults on and builds with AI — specifically with his own text analysis tool [Ressada](https://ressada.com/). On May 6th he'll run the workshop on using n8n and small LLMs to automate experimentation workflows, together with Slobodan Manić.

He also pointed me to [DSPy](https://dspy.ai/) and [MCPo](https://github.com/open-webui/mcpo) — two very useful tools to increase the reliability of your AI workflows. Worth exploring if you're tired of prompt engineering.

The workshop covers:
- Integrating AI into existing workflows without overcomplicating things
- Building efficient pipelines that minimize errors
- Practical experimentation with different models
