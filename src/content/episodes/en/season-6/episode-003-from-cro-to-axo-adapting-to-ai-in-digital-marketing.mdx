Slobodan "Sani" Manic — host of the [No Hacks Podcast](https://www.nohackspod.com/), co-founder of [Web Performance Tools](https://www.webperformancetools.com/) and [PodPacer](https://podpacer.com/), and one of the organizers of [Future of eXperimentation](https://futureofexperimentation.com/) — joins Guido to talk about the shift from Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) to what he calls **Agent Experience Optimization (AXO)**.

His thesis is blunt: your next million visitors won't be human. AI agents are already doing more of the browsing and buying, and the websites that survive the transition will be the ones designed for both humans and machines. Sani's framework, [Machine-First Architecture](https://machinefirstarchitecture.com/), lays out four pillars — Identity, Structure, Content, and Interaction — for building exactly that.

Sani is co-running the pre-event workshop at **Space Academy** on May 6th with Iqbal Ali, on building reliable agents and workflows with n8n and small models. Tickets at [experimentationelite.com](https://experimentationelite.com/).

**Key takeaways:**
- The shift from CRO to AXO isn't optional — it's a question of when, not if
- AI enhances personalization, automates interactions, and surfaces predictive insights
- The job becomes orchestrating AI alongside human judgment, not replacing one with the other
