Are we creating AI solutions or just chasing problems?

It feels like everyone is jumping on the AI bandwagon — that's exactly what this week's [Space Academy](https://experimentationelite.com/) event is about. But let's also acknowledge that many of us are sometimes looking for problems to fit the solutions we've already built, rather than solving real ones.

Research suggests that **60% of companies investing in AI will abandon most of their projects this year** due to inadequate foundations and guardrails. We're in an age of exploration, trying to optimize what we have, yet many of us are left with a closet of abandoned AI ideas.

In this episode, [Juliana Jackson](https://www.linkedin.com/in/julianajackson/) — AI Strategy & Growth Director at Jellyfish, two-time 2025 award winner (Lovie Awards & BIMA), and Professor of Data Storytelling at IHM Business School — joins Guido to dig into the current state of AI experimentation, the common challenges organizations run into, and what a robust framework for successful AI deployment actually looks like.

Juliana will be MCing this week's Space Academy event, organized by [Experimentation Elite](https://experimentationelite.com/).
